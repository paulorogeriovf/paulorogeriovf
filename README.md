### Hydra ERP — Business Management System

> ERP developed to centralize the commercial, financial and operational processes of a swimming pool business.

Web system developed and structured for use in a real business environment.

It provides management of customers, pool professionals and products, sales tracking through sales records, partial payments, billing, commissions, transaction history, quote generation and business indicators.
